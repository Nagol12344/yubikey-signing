How it works:
Your PGP private key is encrypted by a secret only your YubiKey can derive. It never exists unencrypted on disk.
Setup
Create or import your PGP key. It will be sealed to your YubiKey using WebAuthn PRF — only your physical key can unlock it.
Step 1
Register your YubiKey
Creates a WebAuthn credential on your YubiKey. This binds the encryption to this specific key. Touch the gold contact when prompted.
Step 2
Your PGP identity
Generate a new key pair, or import an existing one.
Step 3
Ready to use
Your key is sealed. Share your public key so others can encrypt messages to you.
Compose
Encrypt and sign a message. Recipients need to have their public key in your key ring.
